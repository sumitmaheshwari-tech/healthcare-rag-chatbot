# MedCare General Hospital — Departments

## Cardiology
The Department of Cardiology at MedCare provides comprehensive cardiac care, from preventive screenings to complex interventional procedures. Our team of experienced cardiologists uses the latest diagnostic tools including echocardiography, cardiac catheterization, and electrophysiology studies.
- **Conditions Treated:** Coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, valvular heart disease, hypertension, peripheral vascular disease, congenital heart defects
- **Procedures:** Angioplasty & stenting, coronary angiography, pacemaker implantation, echocardiogram (2D Echo), TMT (Treadmill Test), Holter monitoring, cardiac rehabilitation
- **Contact:** Extension 2001, Block A — 2nd Floor

## Neurology
The Neurology Department specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of disorders of the nervous system, including the brain, spinal cord, and peripheral nerves. Our neurologists are trained in advanced neuroimaging interpretation, EEG analysis, and stroke management.
- **Conditions Treated:** Stroke, epilepsy, migraine and headache disorders, Parkinson's disease, Alzheimer's and dementia, multiple sclerosis, neuropathy, vertigo, brain tumours
- **Procedures:** EEG (Electroencephalogram), EMG/NCV (nerve conduction studies), lumbar puncture, Botox for migraines, thrombolysis for acute stroke
- **Contact:** Extension 3001, Block A — 3rd Floor

## Orthopedics
Our Orthopedics Department offers expert care for musculoskeletal conditions, from fracture management to total joint replacements. We use computer-navigated surgery and minimally invasive techniques for faster recovery.
- **Conditions Treated:** Fractures, osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, ligament tears (ACL/MCL), rotator cuff injuries, spinal disorders, sports injuries, osteoporosis
- **Procedures:** Total knee replacement, total hip replacement, arthroscopic surgery, fracture fixation (ORIF), spinal fusion, PRP therapy, physiotherapy & rehabilitation
- **Contact:** Extension 1001, Block B — 1st Floor

## Dental
The Dental Department provides a full range of dental services from routine checkups to advanced cosmetic and surgical procedures. Our clinic is equipped with digital dental X-ray, intraoral cameras, and CAD/CAM technology.
- **Conditions Treated:** Cavities and tooth decay, gum disease (gingivitis, periodontitis), tooth infection/abscess, malocclusion, TMJ disorders, oral ulcers
- **Procedures:** Root canal treatment, dental implants, teeth whitening, dental crowns & bridges, braces and aligners, tooth extraction, dental scaling & polishing, smile makeover
- **Contact:** Extension 1001, Block C — 1st Floor

## Dermatology
The Dermatology Department addresses all skin, hair, and nail conditions. Our specialists offer both medical dermatology and cosmetic procedures using the latest laser and light-based technologies.
- **Conditions Treated:** Acne, eczema, psoriasis, fungal infections, vitiligo, hair loss (alopecia), skin allergies, warts, skin cancers, pigmentation disorders
- **Procedures:** Laser skin resurfacing, chemical peels, mole and wart removal, PRP for hair loss, Botox and fillers, microdermabrasion, phototherapy (UV treatment), skin biopsy
- **Contact:** Extension 2001, Block C — 2nd Floor

## Pediatrics
Our Pediatrics Department provides compassionate care for infants, children, and adolescents. From newborn screening to adolescent health, our paediatricians ensure comprehensive child health management.
- **Conditions Treated:** Childhood infections, asthma, allergies, growth disorders, developmental delays, nutritional deficiencies, childhood diabetes, ADHD
- **Procedures:** Vaccination & immunization, newborn screening, developmental assessment, allergy testing, nebulization, growth monitoring, paediatric emergency care
- **Contact:** Extension 1001, Block D — 1st Floor

## ENT (Ear, Nose & Throat)
The ENT Department offers specialized care for conditions affecting the ear, nose, throat, and related structures of the head and neck. Our team performs both medical management and advanced surgical procedures.
- **Conditions Treated:** Sinusitis, tonsillitis, hearing loss, ear infections, nasal polyps, deviated septum, sleep apnoea and snoring, voice disorders, vertigo, thyroid nodules
- **Procedures:** Endoscopic sinus surgery (FESS), tonsillectomy, septoplasty, myringotomy (ear tube insertion), cochlear implant, audiometry & hearing tests, microlaryngoscopy
- **Contact:** Extension 2001, Block B — 2nd Floor

## Oncology
The Department of Oncology provides comprehensive cancer care including medical oncology, radiation therapy planning, and palliative care. Our multidisciplinary tumour board reviews every case for personalized treatment plans.
- **Conditions Treated:** Breast cancer, lung cancer, colon/rectal cancer, prostate cancer, blood cancers (leukemia, lymphoma), head and neck cancers, gynaecological cancers
- **Procedures:** Chemotherapy, immunotherapy, targeted therapy, bone marrow biopsy, tumour marker testing, cancer screening programs, pain management, palliative care support
- **Contact:** Extension 4001, Block A — 4th Floor

## General Medicine
The General Medicine Department serves as the first point of contact for most patients. Our physicians handle acute and chronic medical conditions and coordinate referrals to specialist departments when needed.
- **Conditions Treated:** Fever, infections, diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, respiratory infections, urinary tract infections, anaemia, electrolyte imbalances
- **Procedures:** General health checkups, diabetes management, blood pressure monitoring, infectious disease management, preventive health screening
- **Contact:** Extension 1001, Block A — 1st Floor

## Gynecology & Obstetrics
Our OB-GYN Department provides comprehensive women's health services, from routine gynaecological exams to high-risk pregnancy management and minimally invasive gynaecological surgery.
- **Conditions Treated:** PCOS, endometriosis, uterine fibroids, menstrual disorders, infertility, high-risk pregnancy, menopausal symptoms, cervical abnormalities
- **Procedures:** Normal and caesarean delivery, laparoscopic hysterectomy, fertility treatment (IUI), colposcopy, Pap smear, prenatal screening, maternity packages
- **Contact:** Extension 3001, Block D — 3rd Floor

## Ophthalmology
The Eye Department provides complete eye care services from routine vision testing to advanced surgical procedures for cataracts, glaucoma, and retinal diseases.
- **Conditions Treated:** Cataracts, glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, refractive errors (myopia, hypermetropia, astigmatism), dry eye, eye allergies
- **Procedures:** Cataract surgery (phacoemulsification), LASIK/PRK, glaucoma surgery, retinal laser, fundus examination, visual field testing, OCT scan
- **Contact:** Extension 2001, Block D — 2nd Floor

## Radiology & Imaging
The Radiology Department provides state-of-the-art diagnostic imaging services to support clinical decision-making across all departments. All reports are provided within 24 hours (urgent reports within 2 hours).
- **Services:** MRI (3 Tesla), CT Scan (128-slice), Digital X-ray, Ultrasound, Color Doppler, Mammography, DEXA Scan, Fluoroscopy, Interventional Radiology
- **Contact:** Extension 5001, Ground Floor, Block A
