# MedCare General Hospital — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## Appointments

### How do I book an appointment?
You can book an appointment through the following methods:
- **Online:** Use the MedCare chatbot or website (www.medcarehospital.in)
- **Phone:** Call +91-40-2345-6789 (Monday–Saturday, 8 AM–8 PM)
- **Walk-in:** Visit the OPD registration counter on the Ground Floor
- **App:** Download the MedCare Patient App (available on Google Play and App Store)

### Can I book an appointment for the same day?
Yes, same-day appointments are available based on doctor availability. We recommend booking early in the morning for same-day slots. Emergency cases are always accommodated.

### How do I reschedule or cancel an appointment?
You can reschedule or cancel through the chatbot, by calling our helpline, or at the OPD desk. Please cancel at least 24 hours in advance for a full refund of any advance payment.

### What should I bring for my first visit?
- Valid photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, passport, or voter ID)
- Insurance card / policy document (if applicable)
- Referral letter from your current doctor (if any)
- All previous medical reports, prescriptions, and imaging CDs
- List of current medications

## Medical Records

### How do I get a copy of my medical records?
Submit a written request at the Medical Records Department (Room 110, 1st Floor) or through the chatbot. Records are typically provided within 48 hours. Digital copies can be emailed for a nominal fee of ₹200.

### Can I access my lab reports online?
Yes, lab reports are available on the MedCare Patient Portal within 24 hours of the test. You will receive an SMS with a link to view and download your reports.

### How long does MedCare keep my medical records?
We retain all medical records for a minimum of 5 years as per Indian medical regulations. Critical records (surgical, oncology) are retained indefinitely.

## Pharmacy

### Where is the pharmacy located?
The in-house pharmacy is on the Ground Floor near the Main Lobby. It is open 24 hours, 7 days a week.

### Do you offer discounts on medicines?
Admitted patients receive a 10% discount on medicines purchased from our pharmacy. Generic medicine alternatives are always available and recommended.

### Can I get a prescription refill without seeing a doctor?
For chronic medication refills (e.g., blood pressure, diabetes), you can request a refill through the pharmacy if your prescription is less than 3 months old. For new refills, a doctor consultation is required.

## Emergency & Ambulance

### What should I do in a medical emergency?
- Call our Emergency Helpline: +91-40-2345-9999 (24/7)
- Or dial 108 (government ambulance service)
- Drive directly to our Emergency Department entrance (separate from main entrance)
- Our ER team triages all cases and ensures immediate care for critical patients

### Does MedCare have an ambulance service?
Yes, we have 5 Advanced Life Support (ALS) ambulances available 24/7. Call +91-40-2345-9999 to request an ambulance. Average response time within Hyderabad: 15–20 minutes.

## Vaccination & Preventive Care

### What vaccinations do you offer?
We offer all standard vaccinations:
- **Children:** Complete immunization schedule as per IAP guidelines (BCG, DPT, IPV, MMR, Hepatitis, Varicella, etc.)
- **Adults:** Flu vaccine (annual), Hepatitis B, Tetanus booster, Pneumococcal, HPV vaccine
- **Travel vaccines:** Yellow fever, typhoid, meningitis, Japanese encephalitis
- **COVID-19:** All approved boosters available

### Do you offer health checkup packages?
Yes! We offer three packages: Basic (₹2,500), Comprehensive (₹5,500), and Executive (₹12,000). See our Treatments section for details.

## Blood Donation

### Can I donate blood at MedCare?
Yes! Our Blood Bank (Ground Floor, Block A) accepts voluntary blood donations. Donors must be 18–65 years old, weigh ≥50 kg, and be in good health. Walk-in donors welcome Monday–Saturday, 9 AM–4 PM.

## Certificates & Documentation

### Can I get a medical certificate?
Yes, medical certificates (fitness certificate, sick leave certificate, medical fitness for travel/work) can be issued by your consulting doctor. Processing time: same day during OPD hours.

### Do you provide medical reports for visa applications?
Yes, we provide immigration medical examination reports. Please bring your passport and visa application details. Cost: ₹3,000–5,000 depending on the embassy requirements.

## Patient Experience

### How do I provide feedback or file a complaint?
- **Feedback forms** are available at the reception and in each department
- **Online:** Email feedback@medcarehospital.in
- **Phone:** Call the Patient Relations team at Extension 1100
- **In-person:** Visit the Patient Relations Office, Room 108, Ground Floor
- All complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 7 working days

### Is there a second opinion facility?
Yes, MedCare encourages patients to seek second opinions. You can request a consultation with another specialist in the same department or from a different hospital. We will share all your records and imaging to facilitate the process.

## Accessibility & Facilities

### Is the hospital wheelchair accessible?
Yes, MedCare is fully wheelchair accessible with ramps at all entrances, elevators to all floors, accessible restrooms, and wheelchair-accessible parking. Wheelchairs are available free of charge at the main entrance and emergency entrance.

### Is there a cafeteria?
Yes, our cafeteria is on the Ground Floor and is open 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. We serve vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals, snacks, and beverages. Special dietary meals (diabetic, low-salt, etc.) are available for patients on request.

### Do you serve international patients?
Yes, MedCare has an International Patient Services desk that assists with medical visa documentation, airport pickup, interpreter services (available in English, Hindi, Telugu, Urdu, Arabic), accommodation arrangements, and post-treatment follow-up coordination.

## Telemedicine

### Can I consult a doctor online?
Yes, MedCare offers teleconsultation/video consultation for follow-up visits and non-emergency consultations. Book through the chatbot, patient app, or call the helpline. Teleconsultation fee is the same as in-person consultation.

### How does a teleconsultation work?
1. Book a teleconsultation slot
2. Receive a video call link via SMS/email
3. Join the video call at the scheduled time
4. Doctor reviews your case, prescribes medication if needed
5. Prescription and notes are shared digitally
6. Medicines can be delivered to your doorstep (Hyderabad only)
