# MedCare General Hospital — About Us

## Overview
MedCare General Hospital is a premier multi-specialty hospital established in 2005 in the heart of Hyderabad, Telangana. With over 250 beds, 8 state-of-the-art operation theatres, and a team of 100+ specialist doctors, MedCare is committed to providing world-class healthcare services to the community.

## Mission
To deliver compassionate, accessible, and evidence-based healthcare to every patient, leveraging the latest medical technology and a team of dedicated professionals.

## Vision
To be South India's most trusted and innovative healthcare institution, setting new standards in patient care, medical education, and community health.

## Facilities

### Inpatient Facilities
- 250-bed hospital with private, semi-private, and general ward options
- 30-bed ICU (Intensive Care Unit) with advanced ventilator support and round-the-clock intensivist coverage
- 10-bed NICU (Neonatal ICU) for premature and critical newborns
- 8 fully equipped modular operation theatres with laminar airflow

### Diagnostic & Imaging
- Advanced 3T MRI scanner
- 128-slice CT scanner
- Digital X-ray and Fluoroscopy
- High-resolution Ultrasound and Color Doppler
- Mammography unit
- Bone Densitometry (DEXA scan)
- Fully automated pathology laboratory (NABL accredited)
