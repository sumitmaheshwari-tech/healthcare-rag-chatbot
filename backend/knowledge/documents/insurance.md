# MedCare General Hospital — Insurance & Billing Information

## Accepted Insurance Providers
MedCare General Hospital provides cashless and reimbursement facilities for the following insurance companies:

1. **Star Health and Allied Insurance** — All plans accepted, cashless available
2. **ICICI Lombard General Insurance** — Cashless for all group and retail plans
3. **HDFC Ergo Health Insurance** — Cashless available, pre-authorization for planned admissions
4. **Bajaj Allianz General Insurance** — Cashless for Optima and Health Guard plans
5. **New India Assurance** — Government health insurance, cashless for most plans
6. **United India Insurance** — Cashless for Mediclaim and group plans
7. **Max Bupa Health Insurance (Niva Bupa)** — Cashless for all individual and family floater plans
8. **Care Health Insurance (formerly Religare)** — Cashless for Care, Care Advantage, and Care Freedom plans
9. **Aditya Birla Health Insurance** — Cashless available
10. **ManipalCigna Health Insurance** — Cashless for ProHealth and Lifetime plans
11. **Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)** — Government scheme, cashless for eligible beneficiaries (up to ₹5,00,000 per family per year)
12. **Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS)** — Cashless for CGHS beneficiaries
13. **Employees' State Insurance (ESI)** — Referral-based cashless treatment

## Cashless Facility
- Cashless treatment means the insurance company settles the bill directly with the hospital — you don't pay out of pocket (except for non-covered items).
- Available for all listed insurance providers above.
- **TPA Desk:** Room 102, Ground Floor — open Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM.
- For emergency admissions, cashless approval is typically processed within 2 hours.

## Claim Process

### For Planned (Elective) Admission
1. Visit the TPA Desk (Room 102) at least 48 hours before your planned admission.
2. Submit: Insurance card/policy copy, valid photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN), hospital doctor's recommendation letter.
3. The TPA desk will send a pre-authorization request to your insurer.
4. Approval is usually received within 24–48 hours.
5. Upon approval, you can proceed with cashless admission.

### For Emergency Admission
1. Get admitted through the Emergency Department.
2. The TPA desk will process pre-authorization within 2–4 hours.
3. Submit insurance documents to the TPA desk as soon as possible.
4. If cashless is not approved in time, you may pay and claim reimbursement later.

### For Reimbursement Claims
1. Pay the hospital bill in full.
2. Collect: Original bills, discharge summary, investigation reports, prescription copies.
3. Submit the claim to your insurance company within 15–30 days of discharge (check your policy for exact timelines).

## Documents Required for Insurance
- Insurance policy card or certificate
- Valid photo ID (Aadhaar card, PAN card, passport, or voter ID)
- Hospital referral letter (if applicable)
- Previous medical records related to the current condition
- Claim form signed by the treating doctor

## Co-Pay Information
- Co-pay percentages vary by insurance plan. Common co-pay structures:
  - **No co-pay:** Many comprehensive plans (Star Family Health Optima, HDFC Ergo Optima Secure)
  - **10% co-pay:** Some plans for senior citizens or pre-existing conditions
  - **20% co-pay:** Budget plans and plans covering pre-existing diseases from day 1
- Check your policy document or contact the TPA desk for your specific co-pay percentage.

## What Insurance Typically Covers
- Room charges (up to policy limit)
- Doctor consultation fees during admission
- Surgery and OT charges
- Anaesthesia charges
- Medicines and consumables during admission
- Diagnostic tests during admission
- ICU charges
- Pre-hospitalization expenses (30–60 days before admission, varies by policy)
- Post-hospitalization expenses (60–180 days after discharge, varies by policy)

## What Insurance Typically Does NOT Cover
- OPD (outpatient) consultations (unless OPD cover is included)
- Cosmetic and aesthetic procedures
- Dental treatments (unless dental cover is included)
- Spectacles and hearing aids
- Experimental or unproven treatments
- Self-inflicted injuries
- Maternity (unless maternity cover is added)
- Waiting period diseases within the waiting period

## Payment Methods Accepted
- **Cash** — at the billing counter
- **Credit / Debit Cards** — Visa, MasterCard, RuPay accepted
- **UPI** — Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI
- **Net Banking** — All major banks
- **EMI** — Available for bills above ₹50,000 (partnered with Bajaj Finserv, ZestMoney)
- **Cheque / DD** — For corporate and government payments

## Refund Policy
- Refunds for cancelled procedures are processed within 7–10 working days.
- Insurance-related refunds are processed after insurer settles the claim (15–30 days).
- Advance deposits are refundable upon discharge, net of any outstanding charges.
