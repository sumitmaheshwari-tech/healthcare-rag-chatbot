# MedCare General Hospital — Treatments & Procedures

## Cardiac Procedures

### Coronary Angiography
A diagnostic procedure where contrast dye is injected into the coronary arteries to visualize blockages using X-ray imaging. Duration: 30–60 minutes. Preparation: Fasting 6 hours before, blood tests, consent form. Cost: ₹15,000–25,000.

### Angioplasty & Stenting
A minimally invasive procedure to open blocked coronary arteries using a balloon catheter and place a stent to keep the artery open. Duration: 1–2 hours. Hospital stay: 1–2 days. Cost: ₹1,50,000–3,00,000 (depending on stent type).

### Pacemaker Implantation
A small device is implanted under the skin to help regulate heart rhythm. Duration: 1–2 hours. Hospital stay: 2–3 days. Cost: ₹2,00,000–4,00,000.

### Echocardiogram (2D Echo)
A non-invasive ultrasound test that creates images of the heart's structure and function. Duration: 20–30 minutes. No special preparation. Cost: ₹1,500–2,500.

### TMT (Treadmill Test)
A stress test where the patient walks on a treadmill while heart activity is monitored. Duration: 30 minutes. Preparation: Wear comfortable clothing, avoid heavy meals 2 hours before. Cost: ₹1,500–2,000.

## Orthopedic Procedures

### Total Knee Replacement
Surgical replacement of a damaged knee joint with an artificial prosthesis. Duration: 1.5–2 hours. Hospital stay: 5–7 days. Recovery: 6–8 weeks with physiotherapy. Cost: ₹2,50,000–4,00,000.

### Total Hip Replacement
Replacement of the hip joint with an artificial joint. Duration: 1.5–2 hours. Hospital stay: 5–7 days. Cost: ₹2,50,000–4,50,000.

### Arthroscopic Surgery
Minimally invasive surgery using a camera and small instruments to diagnose and treat joint problems (knee, shoulder). Duration: 30–90 minutes. Day-care or 1-night stay. Cost: ₹80,000–1,50,000.

### Fracture Treatment (ORIF)
Open Reduction Internal Fixation — surgical alignment and stabilization of broken bones using plates, screws, or rods. Duration: 1–3 hours depending on fracture. Cost: ₹50,000–2,00,000.

## Dental Procedures

### Root Canal Treatment
Removal of infected pulp from inside a tooth, cleaning, and sealing. Duration: 1–2 visits (45 min each). Cost: ₹3,000–8,000 per tooth.

### Dental Implants
Titanium screw placed in jawbone to replace missing teeth, with a crown on top. Duration: 2 visits (surgical placement + crown, 3-month gap). Cost: ₹25,000–50,000 per implant.

### Teeth Whitening
Professional bleaching to remove stains and brighten teeth. Duration: 60–90 minutes. Cost: ₹5,000–15,000.

### Dental Braces / Aligners
Orthodontic treatment to correct misaligned teeth. Duration: 12–24 months. Cost: Metal braces ₹30,000–50,000; Clear aligners ₹80,000–1,50,000.

## Dermatology Procedures

### Laser Skin Resurfacing
Removes damaged skin layer by layer using a laser to improve texture, wrinkles, and scars. Duration: 30–60 minutes. Recovery: 5–7 days. Cost: ₹10,000–30,000 per session.

### Chemical Peel
Application of a chemical solution to exfoliate and improve skin texture. Duration: 20–30 minutes. Cost: ₹3,000–8,000 per session.

### PRP for Hair Loss
Platelet-Rich Plasma therapy where the patient's own blood is processed and injected into the scalp to stimulate hair growth. Duration: 45–60 minutes. Requires 4–6 sessions. Cost: ₹5,000–10,000 per session.

## General Surgeries

### Appendectomy
Surgical removal of the appendix, usually performed laparoscopically. Duration: 30–60 minutes. Hospital stay: 1–2 days. Cost: ₹50,000–1,00,000.

### Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy (Gallbladder Removal)
Minimally invasive removal of gallbladder using keyhole surgery. Duration: 45–90 minutes. Hospital stay: 1–2 days. Cost: ₹60,000–1,20,000.

### Hernia Repair
Surgical repair of hernia (inguinal, umbilical, incisional) using mesh. Duration: 1–2 hours. Hospital stay: 1–2 days. Cost: ₹50,000–1,50,000.

## Diagnostic Procedures

### MRI Scan
Magnetic Resonance Imaging for detailed images of organs and soft tissues. Duration: 30–60 minutes. Preparation: Remove all metal objects. Cost: ₹5,000–15,000 (depending on body part and contrast).

### CT Scan
Computed Tomography for cross-sectional body images. Duration: 15–30 minutes. Cost: ₹3,000–10,000.

### X-Ray
Quick imaging for bones and chest. Duration: 5–10 minutes. Cost: ₹300–800.

### Ultrasound
Non-invasive imaging using sound waves. Duration: 15–30 minutes. Cost: ₹800–2,500.

### Blood Tests
Comprehensive pathology services including CBC, blood sugar, thyroid panel, lipid profile, liver function, kidney function, HbA1c, vitamin panels. Fasting blood sugar requires 8–12 hour fasting. Costs: ₹200–2,000 depending on test panel.
